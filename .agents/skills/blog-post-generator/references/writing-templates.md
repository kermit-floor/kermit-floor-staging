# Writing Guidance and Optional Outlines

Use for new articles or substantial rewrites. Build from one topic plan and adapt per locale.

## Length and structure

Choose the shortest treatment that answers the reader's question well. A substantial guide may
start around 1,400–1,800 words per locale, but this is an editorial estimate, not a minimum.
A focused explanation can be shorter; a comparison can be longer if the extra detail changes
a decision. Targeted edits have no length requirement.

Add H2/H3 headings when the topic or reader question changes. Use short paragraphs, lists,
checklists, or tables where they make the information easier to use. There is no heading or
word-interval quota. Media selection belongs in `media-workflow.md` when needed.

## Optional EN outline

1. The reader's problem and context
2. Decision criteria
3. Practical guidance and checks
4. Where the relevant SPC products fit, supported by evidence
5. A useful next step

Adapt or omit sections to fit the brief. Include comparisons only for comparison intent.

## Optional TR outline

1. Okuyucunun sorunu ve bağlam
2. Değerlendirme kriterleri
3. Uygulamaya dönük öneriler ve kontroller
4. İlgili SPC ürünlerinin uygun olduğu noktalar
5. Okuyucu için yararlı bir sonraki adım

## Editorial and locale requirements

- Keep tone neutral, practical, and useful to B2B readers.
- Preserve the brief's priority points and examples without padding around them.
- Keep facts, search intent, and the intended next step aligned across locales; localize
  phrasing and examples rather than translating sentence by sentence.
- Use proper Turkish characters (`ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü`).
- Keep article copy reader-facing: turn briefing notes into editorial prose, with no references
  to the user's prompt, supplied list, or drafting process.
- Use bold sparingly for decision-critical terms or cautions.
