## Interference decision before shipping

The same products and English topics may compete for Romanian searches across the
two domains. This affects country-scoped discovery and enquiries even though the
Workers and analytics properties are independent. Treatment: ship and re-baseline
**the Romania country cohort** on September 25, excluding launch day. Use this fresh
.com comparator plus the first .ro post-launch period; review combined cross-domain
Romanian visibility on **November 6**. Do not attribute movement in this cohort solely
to any older change. The .com non-Romania cohorts retain their original baselines
and dates. Aggregate conclusions must either exclude Romania or explicitly report
the launch as a confound and use the November 6 combined comparison.

## Launch hypothesis and review

Romanian language, local DMS contact details and correct .ro metadata should improve
Romanian discoverability and make local enquiries easier. Primary metrics: .ro GSC
Romania impressions/clicks/position and GA4 generate_lead, with .com Romania traffic
as a cross-domain guardrail. Report both domains separately and combined where
comparable; don't infer new demand from traffic merely moving between domains.

Indexing check October 9; interim ranking check October 23; structural/cross-domain
review November 6. Read instrumentation weekly; conversion interpretation no earlier
than October 25 and only with sufficient volume. These are recorded review dates,
not newly scheduled automations.
