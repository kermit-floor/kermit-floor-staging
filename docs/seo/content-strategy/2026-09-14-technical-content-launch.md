## Claim decisions

- The shared specification table covers only Elegance, Sky, Elite, Mosaic and Travertine.
  Their live profiles support 5/6/7 mm, 0.30/0.50 mm, 1/1.5 mm attached IXPE options, formats
  and lock choices. Availability of every possible combination is not implied. The quotation
  must select the configuration and clarify whether the quoted thickness includes backing.
- Natural, Stone and Full Natural are omitted from that comparison because their profiles
  still contain the retired 0.55 mm option. No new article repeats it, and this content batch
  does not change product-page specifications during the pending H1/schema measurements.
- The separate Romanian technical sheet supports the explicitly identified **6.00 mm,
  0.50 mm wear, no attached IXPE, 228.6 × 1219.2 mm, 2,000 kg/m³** example. Acoustic
  performance remains undeclared. The diagram is not a claim about every collection.
- Installation claims use the actual published English manual and the September 13 owner
  decisions: suitable indoor use; no bathroom-floor, outdoor or hose-cleaned applications;
  at least 10 mm clearances; product-specific locking method. The manual supplies the
  2 mm over 1 m flatness criterion. No crack-bridging allowance is repeated. Room-size,
  exact acclimatisation and lock-specific sequences stay in the applicable instructions.
- Heating uses the published EN/TR supplement: finished surface maximum **27°C**, floor
  sensor, approved underlay, gradual 3–5°C/day initial start-up subject to the ceiling,
  drying/commissioning and control of local heat. Electric/infrared and cooling applications
  require the full manual's separate conditions; compatibility is not automatic.
- No new capacity, warranty duration, lifetime, independent certification, energy-saving,
  acoustic, blanket wet-area or scratch-proof claim is introduced. Supplied certificate
  publication and warranty terms remain separate unresolved dependencies.
- Primary external context was checked live on September 14: [MMFA installation bulletin](https://mmfa.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/TB2_Installation-of-MMF-Floor-Coverings_2022-10_EN.pdf)
  and [heating guidance](https://mmfa.eu/en/products/installation/laying/heatingcooling/).
  These support general planning principles, not independent approval of Kermit products.
  Kermit's applicable documents determine its numerical limits.
