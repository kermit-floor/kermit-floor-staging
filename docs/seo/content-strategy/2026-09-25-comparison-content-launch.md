## Editorial evidence

- Actual Kermit installation/care and 6 mm no-IXPE technical PDFs were extracted and read.
  Preserve bathroom-floor, continuously wet, outdoor, pool/sauna and hose-cleaning limits.
  Damp cleaning is not permission for standing water. Water resistance does not replace
  substrate checks, moisture protection or the required floating-floor movement.
- Kermit's example is scoped to the documented 6.00 mm, 0.50 mm wear-layer, no-IXPE
  configuration. Its acoustic field is undeclared. No universal lifespan, installed price,
  decibel value, warranty duration or guaranteed repair is invented. No document is edited.
- Primary pages from RFCI and MMFA were fetched directly September 25 to explain flexible
  versus rigid categories, LVT/LVP formats and the different SPC/WPC naming conventions.
  Their classifications are attributed; the guide does not claim Kermit sells every category.
- EPLF installation and home-care bulletins support a short laminate construction/care
  explanation. Quick-Step's actual laminate page supplies a named example of manufacturer
  water-resistance claims, avoiding the earlier blanket suggestion that laminate is only
  suited to dry rooms. Its product approvals are not transferred to Kermit.
- Existing SPC definition, thickness, installation, underlay, care and room guides receive
  contextual links. Original writing follows the approved topic map, not competitor wording.
  Factory/certificate/capacity topics remain outside this batch's supported scope.
