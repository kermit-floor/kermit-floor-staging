## Evidence and content decisions

- The dealer guide uses the owner's established **one-container wholesale SPC flooring MOQ,
  four-week standard lead time, and OEM/private-label availability**. Exact packing, mix,
  schedule starting point, destination timing and commercial arrangements need an order
  quotation. It does not advertise free samples, guaranteed stock, exclusivity, margins,
  a supplied marketing-pack inventory or a fixed container load.
- Support is described through the actual published Resources library and a request for
  additional materials. Placeholder starter-pack metadata is not treated as an available
  download or a confirmed package of support services.
- The project guide keeps flooring, skirting and wall-panel descriptions separate. The
  standard wall-panel dimensions are scoped to `wall-standard` (4 mm, 960 × 2800/1400 mm).
  Berlin 100 and Optima 90 use their real 2500/2400 mm lengths and other profile dimensions.
  Nominal skirting depth is not claimed to establish movement-gap coverage automatically.
- The published flooring instructions support suitable indoor uses, the bathroom/outdoor/
  hose-cleaning exclusions, and at least 10 mm movement clearances. The owner-approved
  heating guidance supplies the 27°C maximum finished-floor surface temperature. The
  article links the earlier detailed guides and does not replace lock-specific instructions.
- DESIGN uses actual Elite P-204, P-219, P-205; Travertine P-404; and standard wall-panel
  205 membership/names. A shared Buttermilk Oak name is explicitly not proof of a physical
  colour match. The five verified Premier collection profiles supply the format table;
  every possible decor/format combination is not promised. Natural/Stone/Full Natural's
  retired 0.55 mm option is not repeated and their existing product data is not edited.
- Collection interiors illustrate palettes. They are not labelled as client case studies or
  as a controlled colour comparison. No herringbone, stair, embossed-in-register, acoustic,
  fire, slip, load, warranty-duration or independent certification claim is inferred from
  images, a generic product family or an unrelated document.
- The three copyable worksheets are editorial planning tools; they are not product approvals,
  signed specifications or an extra downloadable document product. All source paths were
  checked against files that actually exist. Copy is original, using the approved topic map
  rather than substituting the company name in competitor prose.
