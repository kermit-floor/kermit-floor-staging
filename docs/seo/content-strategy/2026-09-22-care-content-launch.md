## Evidence and editorial decisions

- The actual September installation/care PDF supplies indoor-use limits, 2 mm over 1 m
  flatness, substrate-specific moisture checks, minimum 10 mm movement clearances,
  damp cleaning/mild cleaner, furniture protection and type-W castors under EN 12529.
  Source files are recorded in each post. Product- and lock-specific instructions still apply.
- The 6 mm no-IXPE Romanian technical sheet explicitly leaves acoustic performance
  undeclared. No decibel value, floor sound class or building performance is invented.
  Backing and separate underlay are kept distinct; additional layers need assembly approval.
- MMFA's actual underlay page and October 2022 technical bulletin were fetched directly
  September 22. Short paraphrases explain whole-system selection and impact/walking sound.
  These are general industry guidance, not Kermit certification or product approval.
- Care supports assessment and traceability without inventing a plank-cutting procedure,
  guaranteed replacement, claim deadline, warranty term or supplied warranty PDF. The
  applicable warranty is requested through the supplier. No source document was edited.
- ROOMS preserves the bathroom-floor/outdoor/pool/sauna/continuously-wet/hose-cleaning
  restrictions and the limits on uncontrolled conservatories/unheated holiday homes.
  It keeps flooring and wall-panel application decisions separate without adding links
  to the wall-panel pages currently under experiment.
- The 27°C limit is a finished-floor surface limit. Collection images are illustrations,
  not evidence of installed client projects, acoustic tests or unrestricted commercial use.
  No new certification, factory, manufacturing capacity or retired 0.55 mm claim is added.
