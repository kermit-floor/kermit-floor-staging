Kermit Analytics MCP

An internal reporting integration operated by Kermit Plastik Sanayi ve Ticaret Anonim Şirketi, the company behind Kermit Floor.

The integration connects authorized company users and their AI-assisted workflows to Kermit's Google Ads, Google Analytics and Google Search Console accounts. It helps the team understand website performance, review advertising results and make informed content decisions.

What the integration does

  • Google Ads: retrieves accessible account details and campaign performance reports, including impressions, clicks, costs and conversions.
  • Google Analytics: reviews website traffic, acquisition, engagement and conversion reports, and supports account-owner-authorized measurement configuration.
  • Google Search Console: reviews search queries, page performance and indexing information, and supports account-owner-authorized site administration.

Keyword ideas, search-volume estimates and forecasts are planned additions. They require Google Ads Basic access and a corresponding integration update.

Who uses it

Kermit Analytics MCP is used by authorized Kermit personnel for company accounts. This page describes the integration; it does not offer a public account-registration service. Access to Google data depends on the permissions granted to the integration and the operator.

Report data can be processed by the AI assistant selected by an authorized operator. Saved reports and assistant processing are explained in the application privacy notice.
