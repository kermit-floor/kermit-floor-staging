Application privacy notice

How we handle Google data

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This notice applies to Kermit Analytics MCP, an internal integration operated by Kermit Plastik Sanayi ve Ticaret Anonim Şirketi (Kermit Floor). It describes the integration's Google API data use. The website privacy policy separately explains the handling of website visitor data.

1. Data the integration accesses

  • Google Ads: account identifiers and names, campaign configuration and performance data such as impressions, clicks, costs and conversions.
  • Google Analytics: authorized account and property information, aggregated traffic, acquisition, engagement and conversion reports, and measurement settings when an operator requests configuration work.
  • Google Search Console: authorized site information, search queries, page URLs, clicks, impressions, position and indexing information.
  • Authorization information: Google-issued credentials, tokens and service-account configuration needed to authenticate permitted API requests.

The integration accesses company accounts made available through the operator's Google permissions. It does not request Google account passwords. Keyword-planning data is a planned addition, subject to the required API approval and implementation.

2. How we use the data

We use this data to produce reports, compare reporting periods, investigate traffic and measurement changes, and support Kermit's website, content and advertising decisions. An authorized operator may also request changes to supported measurement or site settings. Google Ads reporting currently uses read-only queries.

We do not sell Google API data, use it to determine creditworthiness, or transfer it to data brokers. This integration does not create personalized advertising audiences from Google user data.

3. AI assistants and service providers

When an authorized operator uses an AI assistant to request analysis, relevant Google API responses and report excerpts can be sent to that assistant's provider to process the request. The resulting analysis can appear in the assistant conversation and in saved project files. The selected provider's applicable service terms, account data controls and retention settings also apply.

Operators must use company-approved services and settings for this purpose. Google API data must not be submitted for training general-purpose AI models. Access by company personnel and service providers is limited to the authorized reporting, support and security purposes, or to disclosures required by law.

4. Storage and retention

Report snapshots and analysis are stored in company-managed workspaces, project records and the assistant conversations used for the work. Saved project reports currently have no automatic expiry; they support historical comparisons and records of decisions. Assistant providers may retain conversation or service records according to the applicable account settings and terms.

Authorization credentials are maintained separately from public website content. Requests to Google use encrypted connections and are subject to Google account and service-account permissions. This public information page does not display account reports or credentials.

5. Revocation and deletion requests

An account owner can stop future access by removing the integration's access in the relevant Google service, withdrawing an applicable Google account authorization, or asking the company administrator to revoke its service-account permissions.

To request deletion of saved reports or analysis, email info@kermit.com.tr and identify the relevant account or report without sending credentials. We will review the request and address records under our control, including any retention required by law. Removing integration records does not delete the original data held in Google Ads, Google Analytics or Google Search Console; records held by an assistant provider may also require a request through that service.

6. Google API data commitments

Kermit Analytics MCP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Material changes to the integration's data use will be reflected in this notice. We will obtain any additional authorization required before using Google data for a new purpose.
