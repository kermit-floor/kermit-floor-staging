**Choose SPC flooring by the conditions in each room, as well as its appearance.** Kermit's installation manual covers indoor, temperature-controlled spaces and identifies kitchens, living spaces and entrance areas as suitable when the selected product and site conditions are appropriate. A room name alone does not establish the right specification.

Start with the activity, cleaning method, moisture exposure, temperature and furniture. Record those needs before choosing a decor. Use this guide alongside the [English installation and care manual](/downloads/installation-guides/kermit-spc-flooring-installation-care-en-2026-09.pdf), then confirm the actual product and installation detail.

## Compare rooms with the same questions

Walk the site with the person specifying or fitting the floor. A shared checklist makes differences visible: one bedroom may have a rolling desk chair, while another has direct afternoon sun; a kitchen may connect to an entrance without a doorway.

| Room or use | Main checks before selection |
|---|---|
| Living room | Furniture loads, sunlight, heating, transitions and the desired visual layout |
| Bedroom | Furniture, any desk-chair castors, acoustic needs and temperature control |
| Kitchen | Spills and leaks, cleaning method, fixed cabinets, appliances and movement space |
| Entrance | Dirt and moisture management, suitable mats, door clearance and cleaning |
| Rental home | Actual room conditions, occupant instructions, product records and spare material |
| Commercial room | Exact product suitability, traffic, loads, cleaning and project evidence |

This is a selection checklist, not blanket approval for every use listed. Confirm the chosen specification for the project. The cover illustration shows a design setting; it is not evidence of a tested application or a project reference.

## Living rooms: plan around furniture and light

Assess the floor with the seating, storage and circulation arrangement in mind. Heavy furniture needs suitable protection and load distribution. The floating floor must retain the movement space required by the installation instructions; fixed furniture and trims must not restrain it.

Large windows add a separate consideration. Plan shading where strong sunlight reaches the floor, and discuss the actual exposure before approving the product. A room that is indoors can still experience conditions outside the intended use, particularly in an uncontrolled conservatory.

Compare physical colour samples where the floor will be used, in daylight and under the room's lighting. The [colour and format guide](/blog/choose-spc-flooring-colour-format) explains how to keep decor codes, sample observations and layout choices together. A screen image helps make a shortlist; it does not settle a colour match.

## Bedrooms: include the desk and acoustic brief

A bedroom can combine sleeping, storage and working. Record wardrobes, a desk, chair movement and heating before treating it as a simple low-traffic room. Choose suitable furniture protection; where castors are used, Kermit's manual specifies appropriate type-W castors under EN 12529.

If sound transmission matters, write down the requirement and ask for evidence for the proposed assembly. The [underlay and acoustics guide](/blog/spc-flooring-underlay-acoustics) separates attached backing, separate underlay and the evidence needed for an acoustic claim. A comfortable feel or a backing material name cannot establish a bedroom's sound performance.

Check rugs and furniture against any heating instructions. For heated floors, Kermit's maximum finished surface temperature is 27°C. Keep the control arrangements in the handover record so the occupant can maintain the agreed conditions.

## Kitchens: coordinate cabinets, appliances and cleaning

Kitchens require a layout and service plan. Identify fixed cabinets and islands before fitting, and agree how the floating floor's movement will be maintained around them. Kermit's manual requires at least 10 mm clearance at walls and fixed objects. Do not allow cabinets, skirting or profiles to pin the floating floor.

Plan appliance access and future movement with suitable protection and load distribution. Also agree how spills will be removed and how leaks will be addressed. Routine care uses a damp cloth or mop with a suitable mild cleaner; the floor is not intended for hosing down or for standing water to remain on it.

The installation sequence should be agreed by the cabinet and flooring installers before work starts. Use the [installation planning guide](/blog/kermit-spc-flooring-installation-planning) to record the prepared base, moisture checks, movement gaps and transitions. Kitchen suitability does not remove those installation requirements.

## Entrances: check the door and the mat

At an entrance, check the finished floor level against door clearance and adjoining surfaces. Include the agreed preparation and underlay, rather than measuring only the plank. Keep any required movement space at thresholds and fixed details.

Select a mat whose contact material is suitable for the flooring. Kermit's care manual warns against rubber and latex materials that can stain the surface. Arrange cleaning so tracked-in dirt and moisture are dealt with promptly, and confirm that the planned method remains within the care instructions.

## Rentals: hand over a maintainable specification

“Rental property” describes ownership and use, not a separate flooring performance class. Assess its kitchens, living areas and bedrooms in the same way as any other home. Occupancy changes make clear records particularly useful.

Keep the collection, decor, specification and batch labels with the property file. Agree spare material when ordering, and give occupants the cleaning method, furniture-protection advice and heating limits. The [care and repair enquiry guide](/blog/kermit-spc-flooring-care-repair) provides a record for identifying the product and describing a concern later.

For a larger refurbishment, use a room schedule showing each finish, specification, transition and document requirement. The [project specification guide](/blog/spc-interior-finishes-project-specification) gives a practical starting point. Treat commercial or unusual uses as a separate specification enquiry with their actual traffic, loads and cleaning conditions.

## Keep excluded conditions out of the room schedule

Kermit's manual **does not recommend bathroom floors, outdoor areas, pools, saunas, continuously wet spaces or areas cleaned by hosing**. It also does not approve uncontrolled conservatories or unheated holiday homes with extreme conditions. Do not turn general SPC water-resistance wording into approval for those settings.

A wall-panel application is a separate product decision. Flooring suitability does not establish wall-panel suitability, and advice for a wall does not approve the floor below it. Confirm the product, substrate and installation method for each surface in its own right.

Before ordering, send the room schedule, selected product, heating information and any special project requirement to [Kermit](/contact). Resolve suitability questions while the specification can still be adjusted, then retain the agreed documents for the installer and occupant.

```faq
## Room-selection questions

### Can Kermit SPC flooring be used in a kitchen?

Kitchens are included in the manual's intended indoor uses when the product and site conditions are appropriate. Confirm preparation, movement around fixed cabinets, appliance protection and the damp-cleaning method.

### Is SPC flooring suitable for a bedroom or living room?

Assess the selected product for the actual indoor conditions, furniture, heating and acoustic needs. Keep within Kermit's installation and care instructions; the room name alone is not a specification.

### Does kitchen suitability also approve bathroom floors?

No. Kermit's manual does not recommend bathroom floors, continuously wet areas or hosed cleaning. Evaluate each room against its stated limits.

### What should a landlord keep after installation?

Keep the order and product details, batch labels, installation records, care instructions and applicable warranty terms. Identify any spare material and pass the cleaning and heating instructions to the occupant.
```
