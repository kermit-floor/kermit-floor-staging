**Yes — SPC wall panels work very well in bathrooms.** The moisture-tolerant panels install over suitable existing surfaces with no grout lines, so renovation runs faster and daily cleaning is easier than with tiled walls.

The deciding factor is correct detailing: the sections below cover where SPC panels fit, what to check before installation, and how to maintain them for the long term.

## Why SPC panels are considered

- Moisture-tolerant material structure
- Fast installation over suitable existing surfaces
- Easy cleaning compared to grout-heavy systems

## Installation checkpoints

1. Confirm substrate is stable and dry.
2. Apply the recommended adhesive system.
3. Seal critical joints and transitions with correct detail accessories.

## Maintenance expectations

SPC panels require routine cleaning with non-aggressive products. Avoid abrasive chemicals and follow project-specific technical guidance.

## Project fit

For bathrooms where fast renovation and consistent visual finish matter, SPC wall panels are a strong option when installed with correct detailing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can SPC wall panels go inside a shower?

Yes. SPC wall panels are used on bathroom walls exposed to splashes and humidity — including shower surroundings — when the substrate is stable and dry and all joints and transitions are finished with the correct detail accessories. For surfaces with continuous direct water exposure, confirm the detailing against the product's technical guidance before installation.

### Do SPC wall panels need grout?

No. SPC panels are installed with a glue-down system that creates a grout-free surface, so there are no grout lines to scrub or re-seal over time. Only critical joints and transitions are finished with the recommended detail accessories.

### Are SPC wall panels faster than tiles for a bathroom renovation?

In most renovations, yes. Large-format panels (960 x 2800 mm) can go over suitable existing surfaces, which removes demolition, tile setting, and grout curing from the schedule. The condition of the existing surface still decides whether overlay installation is appropriate.

### How do SPC wall panels compare to tiles for cleaning?

SPC panels form a continuous, grout-free surface, so routine cleaning is usually limited to wiping with non-aggressive products. Abrasive chemicals should be avoided, as with any finished wall surface.
